# OpenLimb  
## Robotics Without Limits.

OpenLimb is an open-source modular robotics platform designed to accelerate the development of affordable, customizable, and transparent robotic systems.

The project provides an open framework for designing, building, validating, and improving:

- Robotic limbs
- Wearable robotics
- Exoskeleton systems
- Industrial robotic platforms
- Human-machine interfaces
- AI-powered robotic systems

OpenLimb combines open hardware, AI-assisted engineering, legal AI protection, design provenance, and modular manufacturing into a unified robotics ecosystem.

The mission of OpenLimb is to create a global robotics infrastructure where advanced robotic technology can be built, repaired, improved, and shared by communities worldwide.

---

# Vision

Traditional robotics development is often limited by:

- Expensive proprietary systems
- Closed hardware platforms
- Restricted repair options
- Patent barriers
- Limited customization
- Lack of transparent design history

OpenLimb provides an alternative:

**A modular, open, and collaborative robotics ecosystem where innovation remains accessible.**
