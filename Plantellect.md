# 🌱 Plantellect
Your AI-Powered Permaculture Companion! 🧠🌿

**Plantellect** is an open-source, AI-powered gardening and permaculture platform designed to help gardeners of all levels grow resilient, regenerative, and budget-friendly gardens. It combines **plant identification, permaculture design intelligence, conversational AI, and cost-optimization guidance** into a single modular platform.

---

## 🌟 Vision

Plantellect aims to:

- Empower gardeners with AI-driven plant recognition and guidance  
- Promote permaculture and regenerative gardening principles  
- Optimize gardening for budget, resources, and sustainability  
- Provide a contributor-friendly, open-source ecosystem for continuous improvement  

---

## 🧩 Core Features & Modules

### 🌿 Plant Vision & Health AI
- Photo upload & camera capture  
- Species identification (common + scientific names)  
- Edible / medicinal / toxic classification  
- Native vs invasive indicator  
- Plant health & pest/disease detection  
- Confidence scoring & similar species suggestions  

### 🌎 Permaculture Intelligence Engine
- Companion planting recommendations  
- Guild creation (tree-centered ecosystems)  
- Nitrogen fixer identification  
- Pollinator-support & pest-repelling plants  
- Soil-building strategies & water retention  
- Polyculture layout logic  

### 🧠 Conversational Gardening AI
- Natural language Q&A for gardening questions  
- Step-by-step growing instructions  
- Soil prep, composting, pruning & harvesting guidance  
- Organic pest control suggestions  
- Seasonal planting schedules  
- Seed saving & propagation guidance  
- Budget-friendly gardening tips  

### 🪴 Garden Design System
- Drag-and-drop garden layout planner  
- Sun mapping & microclimate integration  
- Food forest layer, raised bed, and container planning  
- Plant spacing & height visualization  

### 💰 Cost Optimization Module
- DIY soil amendment guides  
- Composting & mulching strategies  
- Rainwater harvesting suggestions  
- Propagation from cuttings & seeds  
- Low-cost material alternatives  

### 📓 Garden Journal System
- Photo timeline of plant growth  
- Plant growth & yield tracking  
- Notes & observations logging  
- Seasonal performance history  

### ❓ AI Help Section
- Interactive AI assistant for all features  
- Explains tech stack, modules, and plant recommendations  
- Provides guidance for contributions and improvements  
- Offers insight into future upgrades and possibilities  

---

## 🗄️ Plant Database Requirements
- Botanical + common names  
- Hardiness zones, light, water, soil preferences  
- Companion & antagonistic plants  
- Pollinator value & nitrogen-fixing ability  
- Root depth, height & spread  
- Harvest season, edible parts, medicinal uses  
- Toxicity warnings

## 🔧 Tech Stack
- Open-source computer vision model (e.g., TensorFlow, PyTorch)  
- Open-source NLP / conversational AI framework (e.g., Hugging Face Transformers)  
- Frontend framework (e.g., React, Vue.js)  
- Backend framework (e.g., Django, FastAPI, Node.js)  
- Database system (e.g., PostgreSQL, MongoDB)  
- Mapping & environmental APIs (open-source / free)  
- Modular architecture for community contributions
