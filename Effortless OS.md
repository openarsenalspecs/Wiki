# Effortless OS

The operating system for removing unnecessary work.

Design your life by subtraction.

---

## Overview

Effortless OS is an open-source AGPL-3.0+ decision intelligence system that models time, effort, income, and cognitive load to determine what work should be removed, delegated, automated, or retained.

It functions as a personal and business operating layer that continuously evaluates tasks and life structures through a time-value and effort-reduction framework.

---

## Core Philosophy

Effortless OS is built on four principles:

- Time has measurable economic value
- Not all work is worth doing personally
- Efficiency without identity awareness creates imbalance
- Subtraction is a design strategy, not loss

---

## Core Features

### 1. Hourly Value Engine

Calculates real effective hourly rate:

- Annual revenue normalization 
- Multi-income stream blending
- Adjusted work-hour recalibration
- Optional cost-of-living weighting

Outputs:
- True hourly value
- Value volatility score
- Time-to-income ratio

---

### 2. [Task Cost Analyzer](https://gitlab.com/Roxanne_Ardary/effortless-os/-/blob/0cd2944e6050815e7503716cb4a5f0a24a2febca/Task%20Cost%20Analyzer.md)

Evaluates every task using:

- Time cost
- Cognitive load
- Context switching penalty
- Delay cost
- Opportunity cost

Outputs:
- Task value score
- Outsourcing recommendation
- Automation eligibility
- Elimination candidates

---

### 3. Delegation Engine

Determines optimal execution method:

- Do it yourself
- Delegate to human
- Automate via software
- Eliminate entirely

Includes:
- outsourcing cost estimation
- setup overhead calculation
- break-even analysis

---

### 4. Automation Layer

Identifies tasks suitable for:

- AI workflows
- no-code systems
- scripting pipelines
- recurring template automation

Tracks:
- automation ROI
- time recovery rate
- maintenance cost

---

### 5. Identity Retention System

Ensures life optimization does not erase meaningful activities.

Tracks:

- identity value score
- enjoyment index
- grounding activities
- voluntary inefficiency allowance

Prevents over-optimization drift.

---

### 6. [Friction Index Engine](https://gitlab.com/Roxanne_Ardary/effortless-os/-/blob/0c99006516fb3f3a4f95816dc2df07c752358092/Friction%20Index%20Engine.md)

Measures resistance in daily workflows:

- administrative overhead
- decision fatigue load
- interruption frequency
- task switching cost

Outputs:
- friction hotspots
- system bottlenecks
- simplification targets

---

### 7. Time Debt Tracker

Models deferred or avoided work that compounds over time:

- backlog accumulation
- stress amplification
- compounding inefficiency cost

Outputs:
- time debt score
- priority resolution queue

---

### 8. Regret Modeling System

Estimates future emotional and identity impact:

- probability of future regret
- missed experience value
- long-term satisfaction impact

Outputs:
- regret risk rating
- keep vs remove recommendation adjustment

---

### 9. Life Simulation Mode (Forked Life Engine)

Simulates multiple life trajectories based on decision policies.

Scenarios include:

- Full self-execution model
- Moderate delegation model
- Aggressive automation model
- Identity-preserving optimization model
- Maximum efficiency model

Each simulation produces:

- time freedom projection
- income trajectory impact
- stress index evolution
- identity retention score
- life satisfaction estimate

Includes:

- 6 month, 2 year, and 10 year projections
- compounding effect modeling
- shock event simulation (income loss, burnout, workload spikes)
- forked life branching system

---

### 10. Shock Simulation Layer

Tests system resilience under disruption:

- income reduction scenarios
- workload spikes
- health or time constraints
- automation failure events

Outputs:
- resilience score
- recovery time estimate
- system vulnerability map

---

### 11. Decision Timeline Engine

Tracks decisions over time:

- monthly optimization tracking
- compounding efficiency gains
- behavioral drift detection

Outputs:
- optimization curve
- improvement trajectory
- stagnation detection

---

### 12. Personal Rules Engine

Allows user-defined constraints such as:

- “Never outsource cooking”
- “Keep physical labor tasks”
- “Limit meetings per day”

Ensures system respects personal philosophy overrides.

---

### 13. Attention Cost Model

Evaluates cognitive burden beyond time:

- focus fragmentation rate
- interruption penalty
- recovery time cost

Outputs:
- attention heatmap
- deep work preservation suggestions

---

## System Outputs

Effortless OS generates:

- Task elimination recommendations
- Delegation plans
- Automation roadmaps
- Life simulation comparisons
- Weekly optimization reports
- Time-value dashboards
