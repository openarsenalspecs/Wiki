# VeritasChain Specification

### Where Evidence Becomes Intelligence

## Overview

VeritasChain is an open specification for modular AI systems designed to transform verified evidence into explainable long-term causal intelligence.

Current AI systems are optimized primarily for prediction:

"What happens next?"

VeritasChain focuses on a deeper question:

"What decisions, events, laws, economic conditions, technological changes, and institutional actions created today's reality?"

VeritasChain defines an architecture for AI systems that analyze cause and effect across years, decades, and generations by connecting:

- Legal records
- Court decisions
- Regulations
- Economic changes
- Research and development
- Business decisions
- Technology evolution
- Social impacts
- Infrastructure changes
- Institutional decisions

VeritasChain creates a foundation for AI systems that do not simply generate answers, but build evidence-backed explanations of how outcomes emerge.

# Core Architecture

# 1. Evidence Foundation Layer

The Evidence Foundation Layer provides the verified knowledge base for all causal reasoning.

Supported evidence types include:

- Legal documents
- Scientific publications
- Economic records
- Historical archives
- Corporate records
- Government publications
- Industry reports
- Technical documentation

Features:

- Source verification
- Document version tracking
- Provenance metadata
- Evidence confidence scoring
- Archive synchronization

---

# 2. Temporal Intelligence Engine

The Temporal Intelligence Engine organizes information across time.

Tracks:

- Events
- Decisions
- Policies
- Regulations
- Market changes
- Research milestones
- Technology adoption
- Institutional changes

Each timeline object includes:

- Date
- Source references
- Related entities
- Geographic scope
- Industry impact
- Causal relationships

---

# 3. Causal Graph Engine

The Causal Graph Engine creates interconnected models of cause and effect.

Supports:

- Direct causes
- Indirect causes
- Delayed consequences
- Feedback loops
- Multi-factor explanations
- Competing hypotheses

Example relationship:

Decision

↓

Policy Change

↓

Investment Shift

↓

Technology Development

↓

Market Transformation

↓

Collective Outcome

---

# 4. Long-Term Consequence Engine

Analyzes delayed effects across:

- Years
- Decades
- Generations

Examples:

- Education decisions affecting workforce capability.
- Infrastructure decisions affecting future reliability.
- Research investments creating future industries.
- Regulatory changes influencing innovation.

---

# 5. Counterfactual Analysis Engine

Explores alternative scenarios.

Capabilities:

- Alternative decision paths
- Historical comparisons
- Risk analysis
- Opportunity analysis
- Policy impact modeling

Questions supported:

- What if this decision had not occurred?
- What if this investment happened earlier?
- What if a regulation changed differently?

---

# 6. Causal Confidence Engine

Evaluates the strength of causal relationships.

Measures:

- Evidence quality
- Source reliability
- Number of supporting records
- Conflicting evidence
- Unknown variables
- Alternative explanations
