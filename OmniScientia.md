# OmniScientia

## Understanding nature through curiosity and evidence.

A modular AI framework for scientific discovery, adaptive learning, simulation, and exploration across all fields of science.

---

# Overview

OmniScientia is an open specification for building AI-assisted scientific discovery systems that transform the way people learn, explore, and understand the natural world.

The specification combines artificial intelligence, scientific methodology, laboratory exploration, experiment simulation, verified research retrieval, and adaptive education into a modular framework designed for learners, educators, researchers, citizen scientists, and lifelong explorers.

Instead of presenting science as a fixed sequence of information, OmniScientia creates an evolving discovery environment where questions lead to investigations, investigations lead to experiments, and experiments lead to deeper understanding.

The system guides learners through:

- Asking questions
- Forming hypotheses
- Testing ideas
- Exploring simulations
- Conducting safe experiments
- Reviewing evidence
- Connecting scientific fields
- Creating new discovery pathways

OmniScientia is designed as a foundation for scientific education systems spanning chemistry, biology, physics, Earth sciences, environmental sciences, veterinary science, astronomy, materials science, and future scientific disciplines.

# Core Principles

## Curiosity Before Answers

OmniScientia uses inquiry-driven learning.

The AI encourages students to ask:

- Why does this happen?
- What evidence supports this?
- How could we test it?
- What variables influence the outcome?
- What other scientific fields connect to this discovery?

The goal is developing scientific reasoning rather than memorizing information.

---

## Evidence-Based Discovery

Scientific information is supported through:

- Peer-reviewed research
- Universities
- Government scientific organizations
- Research institutions
- Scientific databases
- Verified educational resources

The system explains:

- Sources
- Methods
- Confidence levels
- Scientific consensus
- Research limitations

---

## Modular Scientific Architecture

Every scientific discipline can exist as an independent module while connecting through shared AI infrastructure.

Modules can be added, replaced, or expanded without redesigning the entire system.
