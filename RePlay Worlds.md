# RePlay Worlds

**From imagination to movie, in a click.**

RePlay Worlds is an open-source, AI-assisted imagination platform where children become authors, artists, composers, directors, and world builders. Rather than consuming pre-written stories, children create original characters, magical worlds, adventures, music, animations, and complete cinematic experiences from their own imagination.

Every adventure grows with the child through persistent save points, branching storylines, procedural world generation, dynamic music, and full movie replay. RePlay Worlds is designed to encourage creativity, curiosity, empathy, critical thinking, and storytelling while maintaining a privacy-first architecture that never treats children as products.

---

## Vision

RePlay Worlds exists to become the world's most advanced creative storytelling platform for children.

Instead of teaching children how to consume entertainment, RePlay Worlds teaches them how to create it.

Every child should be able to imagine a world, build it, explore it, direct it, and replay it as a living movie.
