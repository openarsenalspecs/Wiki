# CortexCFO

## From Historical Data to Future Strategy

CortexCFO is an **AGPL-3.0+ locally hosted AI CFO platform** designed to transform historical financial data into strategic business intelligence. It provides small businesses with private, self-hosted financial forecasting, expense intelligence, risk detection, and AI-powered decision support without requiring financial data to leave their infrastructure.

CortexCFO bridges the gap between accounting records and strategic decision-making by analyzing financial patterns, predicting future outcomes, identifying opportunities, and helping business owners understand the financial impact of their choices.

Unlike traditional accounting software that primarily records what has already happened, CortexCFO focuses on understanding what is likely to happen next.

---

# Vision

Small businesses often have access to financial data but lack the resources of a dedicated CFO, financial analyst, or business intelligence team.

CortexCFO provides an open-source AI financial intelligence layer that helps businesses:

- Understand their current financial position
- Predict future expenses and cash flow
- Identify financial risks before they become problems
- Discover opportunities for improvement
- Simulate business decisions before taking action
- Maintain control of sensitive financial information
