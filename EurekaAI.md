# EurekaAI
**Science Without Limits**

An open-source AI-powered scientific research intelligence platform that analyzes scientific literature, identifies knowledge gaps, generates novel hypotheses, and assists researchers in discovering the next generation of scientific breakthroughs.

---

# Vision

EurekaAI is designed to become an open scientific discovery platform that helps researchers navigate the ever-growing body of scientific knowledge. By combining large-scale literature analysis, knowledge graphs, natural language processing, and AI reasoning, EurekaAI identifies unanswered questions, conflicting evidence, and unexplored research opportunities across every scientific discipline.

Rather than replacing scientists, EurekaAI functions as an intelligent research partner that accelerates discovery, improves literature exploration, and helps generate innovative research directions grounded in existing evidence.

---

# Objectives

- Accelerate scientific discovery
- Identify knowledge gaps automatically
- Generate novel research hypotheses
- Improve literature review workflows
- Encourage interdisciplinary research
- Support reproducible science
- Build an extensible open-source research ecosystem

---

# Architecture

EurekaAI uses a modular architecture that allows every major capability to operate independently while integrating seamlessly with the rest of the platform.

Core functionality remains lightweight and extensible while additional capabilities can be installed as optional plug-ins.
