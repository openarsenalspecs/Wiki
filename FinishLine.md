# FinishLine

**The Smarter Way to Pay Off Debt.**

FinishLine is an AGPL-3.0+ licensed AI-powered debt analysis and optimization platform designed to help users identify the fastest and most cost-effective path to becoming debt free. The system analyzes debt instruments, loan contracts, interest rates, repayment schedules, prepayment penalties, and financial obligations to create intelligent payoff strategies tailored to each user's situation.

Unlike traditional budgeting tools, FinishLine focuses on debt elimination optimization. By combining contract analysis, financial forecasting, and AI-driven recommendations, the platform helps users understand the true cost of their debts and make informed decisions that reduce interest expenses and accelerate financial freedom.
