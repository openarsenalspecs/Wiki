# LightThroughWords

**Turning Ancient Words Into Present Insight**

LightThroughWords is an open-source platform that analyzes religious, philosophical, and historical writings as reflections of human psychology rather than systems of doctrine. By combining natural language processing, historical context, authorship analysis, and semantic reasoning, the platform identifies recurring patterns of human thought, emotion, resilience, and transformation across civilizations.

Rather than asking whether a text is true or false, LightThroughWords asks what the text reveals about the people who wrote it, the challenges they faced, and the psychological strategies they developed to overcome adversity. Every chapter is treated as a unique expression of its author, recognizing that different authors, cultures, and historical periods contribute distinct perspectives within the same body of literature.

The project is designed for researchers, educators, developers, psychologists, historians, and anyone interested in exploring humanity's written history through a transparent, open-source framework.

---

# Project Goals

- Study historical writings through psychology and behavioral science.
- Preserve cultural and historical context.
- Analyze authorial voice instead of assuming uniform perspectives.
- Identify universal patterns of resilience across civilizations.
- Encourage reflection without promoting religious or ideological positions.
- Provide transparent, reproducible, and explainable analyses.

# System Architecture

LightThroughWords follows a modular architecture. Every major capability is implemented as an independent module that can evolve without affecting the rest of the platform.

## Core Modules

### Text Ingestion

Responsible for importing and normalizing historical texts.

Features

- Multi-format document import
- Canonical text management
- Translation management
- Version comparison
- Metadata extraction
- Citation indexing
- Text normalization
