A modular AGPL 3.0+ governance intelligence platform for analyzing business structures, modeling liability, detecting systemic fragility, and simulating economic outcomes under real-world constraints.

---

## Overview

The Distributed Economic Governance System (DEGS) is an open-source infrastructure layer for economic accountability, structural analysis, and governance simulation.

It is designed to:
- analyze business and organizational structures
- map liability and accountability chains
- simulate economic outcomes and systemic risk
- detect fragility and collapse conditions
- support transparent ownership transformation
- enable jurisdiction-aware legal and compliance reasoning

## System Architecture

The system is built as a modular graph-based runtime:

- Core runtime engine
- Ontology graph system
- AI orchestration layer
- Simulation engine
- Rules and policy engine
- Audit ledger system
- Plugin module manager
- Explainability framework

## Design Principles

- Every decision must be traceable
- Every recommendation must be explainable
- Every structure must be auditable
- Every system must support accountability over abstraction
- Simulation replaces assumption wherever possible
