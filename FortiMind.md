# FortiMind
**Your Ethical and Encrypted Decision Partner**
- HTML Mirror:  [https://roxanneardary.com/fortimind-specification/](https://roxanneardary.com/fortimind-specification/)

---

FortiMind is an open-source, human-governed AI decision intelligence framework designed to redefine high-stakes decision support through **ethics, constitutional awareness, transparency, security, and absolute human authority**.

FortiMind is built around one foundational rule:

**AI may analyze, simulate, evaluate, and provide recommendations, but no decision or action may proceed without explicit human authorization.**

---

# Mission & Vision

FortiMind exists to create a new generation of AI systems focused on:

- Human authority over automated decisions
- Ethical reasoning before action
- Constitutional and legal awareness
- Transparency and accountability
- Civilian protection and harm reduction
- Peace-first and de-escalation approaches
- Secure encrypted intelligence workflows
- Open-source verification and public accountability

FortiMind is not designed to replace human judgment.

It is designed to **strengthen human decision-making with secure, transparent, and accountable intelligence support**.

---

# Core Design Principles

## Human Authority First

FortiMind cannot independently execute decisions.

Every recommendation requires:

- Human review
- Human approval
- Recorded authorization
- Secure audit logging

---

## Ethics By Default

Every recommendation must pass through:

- Ethical analysis
- Risk evaluation
- Legal review
- Constitutional review
- Human oversight

---

## Constitutional Awareness

FortiMind continuously evaluates decisions for:

- Constitutional conflicts
- Civil liberty concerns
- Due process concerns
- Legal uncertainty
- Potential government overreach

---

## End-to-End Encryption Everywhere

All system components use encrypted communication and storage.

Protection applies to:

- AI reasoning outputs
- Data pipelines
- Audit records
- Human approvals
- Logs
- Communications
- Plugin interactions

---

# Modular Architecture

FortiMind is designed as a modular framework.

The **Core Modules** provide mandatory safety, governance, encryption, and reasoning capabilities.

**Optional Plugin Modules** extend functionality without compromising the foundational safeguards.

---

# Core Modules

## 1. Ethical Decision Intelligence Core

Provides the foundation for responsible AI-assisted decisions.

Features:

- Pros and cons analysis
- Multi-factor decision evaluation
- Risk scoring
- Ethical impact assessment
- Human-readable explanations
- Alternative option generation
- Decision uncertainty analysis
- Non-violent solution prioritization
- De-escalation recommendations
- Ethical ambiguity detection

---

## 2. Human Governance & Consent Engine

Ensures humans maintain complete authority.

Features:

- Mandatory human approval workflow
- Multi-person approval requirements
- Role-based authorization
- Consent validation
- Approval history tracking
- Automatic pause when oversight is unavailable
- No autonomous execution capability

---

## 3. Constitutional Compliance Engine

Monitors decisions for constitutional concerns.

Features:

- Constitutional risk analysis
- Civil liberty impact review
- Rights-based evaluation
- Potential violation detection
- Constitutional justification reports
- Human review requirements
- Compliance scoring

## 4. Legal Reasoning & Grey Area Detection Engine

Identifies legal uncertainty and provides guidance.

Features:

- Regulatory analysis
