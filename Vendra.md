# Vendra

## The Operating System for Smart Vending

Vendra is an open-source, modular operating system for smart vending infrastructure. Built under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+)**, Vendra provides a complete platform for managing connected vending machines, inventory, payments, analytics, AI optimization, and customer experiences.

Vendra transforms traditional vending machines into intelligent, connected infrastructure by providing operators, businesses, and communities with an extensible platform designed for transparency, interoperability, and data ownership.

## Vision

Vendra creates an open ecosystem for automated retail where operators maintain control over their machines, data, integrations, and technology stack.

The platform enables:

- Independent vending operators
- Universities and campuses
- Hospitals
- Hotels
- Airports
- Government facilities
- Workplaces
- Smart communities
- Automated retail networks
