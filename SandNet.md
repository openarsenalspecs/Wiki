# SandNet

**Build Worlds. Share Adventures. Own Your Data.**

SandNet is a privacy-first, modular, open source game creation and virtual world platform licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 or later (AGPL-3.0+)**. Built as a modern alternative to closed gaming ecosystems, SandNet empowers creators, families, educators, organizations, and communities to build, play, learn, collaborate, and share while maintaining control over their data.
