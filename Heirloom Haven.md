# Heirloom Haven

**Your Garden, Our Legacy.**

Heirloom Haven is an open-source, community-driven seed exchange platform dedicated to preserving native, non-GMO, organic, and heirloom seed biodiversity. Built under the **AGPL-3.0+ license**, it enables individuals and communities to share, trade, and preserve seeds while maintaining transparency, provenance tracking, and ecological awareness.

---

## 🌱 Mission

To preserve agricultural biodiversity by connecting growers, gardeners, and seed stewards through an open, transparent, and locally adaptable seed exchange network.

---

## 🌿 Core Features

### 🧑‍🌾 User & Community System
- User accounts with optional privacy-first profiles  
- Garden profiles (growing zones, interests, specialization)  
- Contribution tracking (seeds shared, exchanged, or requested)  
- Reputation and trust scoring system  
- Optional verification badges for experienced seed stewards  

---

### 🌾 Seed Catalog & Listings
- Create and manage seed listings  
- Detailed seed metadata:
  - Common and scientific names  
  - Variety and lineage (heirloom tracking)  
  - Native region/origin  
  - Growing zones and climate compatibility  
  - Seasonality and germination data  
  - Harvest notes and cultivation tips  
- Tags for ecological roles:
  - Pollinator-friendly  
  - Drought-tolerant  
  - Medicinal  
  - Native preservation priority  
- Photo uploads for seeds and plants  
- Availability states (available, requested, shared, archived)  

---

### 🔍 Search & Discovery
- Advanced search engine integration (Meilisearch / Elasticsearch)  
- Filters by:
  - Region and climate zone  
  - Plant type (vegetable, fruit, herb, flower, tree)  
  - Native vs heirloom classification  
  - Ecosystem role  
- Map-based regional seed discovery  
- Seasonal and trending seed suggestions  

---

### 🔄 Seed Exchange System
- Peer-to-peer seed requests  
- Seed-for-seed swap functionality  
- Donation-based sharing mode  
- Wishlist system for rare seeds  
- Automated matching based on compatibility and region  
- Full exchange history transparency  

---

### 📦 Logistics Support (Optional)
- Eco-friendly packaging guidance  
- Seed-safe shipping instructions  
- Region-based shipping estimates  
- Delivery preference settings  
- Exchange completion tracking  

---

### 🧬 Provenance & Integrity System
- Seed lineage tracking (origin → grower history)  
- Community validation of native/heirloom status  
- Flagging system for hybrids or uncertain origin  
- Image-assisted plant identification (AI support)  
- Edit history logs for transparency  
- Confidence scoring system for seed authenticity  

---

### 🤖 AI Assistance Layer (Open Source)
- Plant identification from images  
- Seed classification suggestions  
- Climate-based growing recommendations  
- Companion planting suggestions  
- Invasive species and climate mismatch warnings  
- Fully transparent and replaceable models  

---

### 📚 Education & Knowledge Base
- Seed saving tutorials  
- Regional planting calendars  
- Soil health and composting guides  
- Companion planting charts  
- Pollinator garden education  
- Community-written wiki articles  

---

### 💬 Community Features
- Discussion forums (Discourse-compatible)  
- Seed storytelling (cultural and historical context)  
- Regional gardening groups  
- Direct messaging between users  
- Community events (seed swaps, planting days)  
- Ratings and feedback on seeds and contributors  

---

### 🗺️ Ecological Mapping
- Interactive biodiversity maps  
- Native species distribution visualization  
- Climate zone overlays  
- Regional seed availability heatmaps  
- Ecosystem restoration tracking tools  

---

### 🧰 Developer & Open Source Features
- Fully self-hostable Docker deployment  
- Modular architecture (service-based design)  
- Public API for:
  - Seed data access  
  - Exchange system integration  
  - Regional datasets  
- Plugin system for community extensions  
- Git-based contribution workflow  
- CI/CD templates for GitLab and Codeberg  

---

### 🔐 Privacy & Security
- Minimal data collection by design  
- Encrypted sensitive user data  
- Optional anonymous usage mode  
- Anti-spam and fraud prevention tools  
- Audit logs for seed and profile changes  
- Self-host transparency by design
