# Alpha Risk Review

Where assumptions become stress-tested outcomes.

Alpha Risk Review is an AGPL 3.0+ modular adversarial intelligence system that evaluates business plans by identifying structural weaknesses, simulating failure scenarios, and determining both what must go right for success and what will likely break first under real-world conditions.

It transforms business ideas into **stress-tested, structured outcomes** through adversarial analysis, simulation, and risk intelligence modeling.

---

# 🧠 Core Concept

Every business plan contains hidden assumptions.

Alpha Risk Review treats those assumptions as:
- testable variables
- failure points under stress
- inputs to adversarial simulation

The system answers:

- What will break first?
- How will it fail?
- What must be true for it to succeed?
- How confident are we in this assessment?

---

# ⚙️ Core Features

## 🧱 Business Schema Normalization Engine
- Converts unstructured business plans into structured representations
- Extracts:
  - value proposition
  - revenue streams
  - cost structure
  - customer segments
  - acquisition channels
  - dependencies
  - regulatory constraints

---

## 🧬 [Business Archetype Classifier](https://gitlab.com/Roxanne_Ardary/alpha-risk-review/-/blob/b863c243196ead4be8b34ab24d0d0ca762d2bf7f/Business%20Archetype%20Classifier.md)
- Identifies business type:
  - SaaS, Marketplace, Consumer App, Infrastructure, Service, Regulated, Hybrid
- Detects subtypes (e.g., B2B SaaS, two-sided marketplace)
- Assigns structural properties:
  - scalability constraints
  - churn sensitivity
  - capital intensity
  - network effect dependency
- Dynamically adjusts risk model weighting

## ⚠️ Risk Intelligence Modules

### Financial Risk Module
- CAC vs LTV imbalance
- burn rate instability
- pricing fragility
- revenue concentration

### Market Risk Module
- demand volatility
- competitive saturation
- timing sensitivity

### Operational Risk Module
- scaling bottlenecks
- hiring constraints
- infrastructure limitations

### Dependency Risk Module
- third-party API reliance
- vendor lock-in exposure
- platform dependency risk

### Regulatory Risk Module
- compliance exposure
- licensing constraints
- geographic restrictions

---

## 🧠 Adversarial Intelligence Layer

### Adversarial Investor Mode
Simulates VC / PE / acquisition-level due diligence thinking:
- generates rejection thesis
- identifies fatal objections
- produces investor “kill questions”
- stress-tests pitch assumptions

---

## 🧨 [Silent Failure Pattern Library](https://gitlab.com/Roxanne_Ardary/alpha-risk-review/-/blob/c22903efbd29b9571abcca9811e188e536be7385/Silent%20Failure%20Pattern%20Library.md)
A continuously evolving knowledge base of hidden failure archetypes:
- growth-decay masking
- unit economics drift
- dependency compression risk
- artificial market fit

Used to:
- match structural patterns
- apply historical failure multipliers
- improve long-term predictive accuracy

---

## 🧨 Failure Simulation Engine
Runs adversarial stress scenarios:
- market shocks
- cost shocks
- dependency failures
- regulatory changes

Outputs:
- collapse timelines
- point-of-no-return detection
- failure cascade chains
- severity scoring

---

## 🧪 “What Would Make This Succeed?” Engine
Inverse analysis system that identifies:
- required success conditions
- retention thresholds
- acquisition efficiency requirements
- distribution constraints
- breakout dependencies

---

## ⚖️ Confidence Calibration Layer
- Normalizes confidence across modules
- Detects missing or inferred data
- Resolves contradictions
- Penalizes assumption-heavy reasoning
- Outputs calibrated confidence score

---

## 📊 Risk Heatmap Generator
- Multi-dimensional risk visualization:
  - financial exposure
  - market fragility
  - operational instability
  - dependency concentration
  - regulatory risk
- Produces structured heatmaps and severity mapping

---

## 🔁 Core Orchestrator
Executes full modular pipeline:

1. Business normalization
2. Archetype classification
3. Risk module execution
4. Adversarial investor analysis
5. Failure simulation
6. Success condition analysis
7. Confidence calibration
8. Risk heatmap generation

Aggregates into final **Alpha Risk Review Report**
