# LumenKinetic Network

**Built from modules. Powered by motion.**

LumenKinetic Network is a modular piezoelectric surface energy and data intelligence system designed to harvest mechanical motion (footsteps, vibration, structural pressure) and convert it into distributed electrical energy and structured real-time telemetry.

It functions as both:
- A **kinetic energy harvesting infrastructure**
- A **distributed environmental intelligence network (“energy as data”)**

---

## Overview

The system is built as a scalable grid of modular tiles that capture mechanical energy through piezoelectric conversion. Each tile acts as both an energy harvester and a sensing node, contributing to a larger distributed network capable of powering low-load systems while simultaneously generating environmental intelligence.

# 🟣 9. Energy as Data (Core Intelligence Layer)

LumenKinetic Network treats every mechanical interaction as both:
- A physical energy event
- A structured data signal

---

## Energy Event Model

Each interaction generates a structured event:

- Timestamp
- Tile ID
- Force magnitude
- Energy generated (Joules)
- Contact duration
- Vibration signature
- Spatial grid position

👉 Each step becomes a **data packet**

---

## Energy Informatics System

- Converts motion into environmental intelligence
- Maps human movement patterns via pressure signatures
- Detects occupancy density without cameras or biometrics
- Identifies high-traffic and low-traffic zones
- Builds spatial usage intelligence models

---

## Distributed Energy Intelligence Network

- Tiles act as both sensors and generators
- Clusters function as local intelligence nodes
- Entire system operates as a distributed sensing mesh

System learns:
- Movement flow patterns
- Energy yield hotspots

## Dual Output Architecture

Each physical event produces:
- 🔋 Electrical energy output

## Energy-to-Information Pipeline

1. Mechanical force applied
2. Piezoelectric impulse generated
3. Electrical signal digitized
4. Structured as ETS event
5. Stored and streamed in real time
6. Used for:
   - Energy optimization
   - Spatial intelligence
   - Infrastructure analytics
