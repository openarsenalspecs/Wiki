# Lexara

Lexara is a jurisdictional intelligence and compliance structuring system that translates state and federal legal rules into executable compensation models. It analyzes referral, marketing, and builder payment structures and generates compliant, risk-scored contract frameworks powered by a rule-aware AI engine and smart contract execution layer.

**Lexara’s core principle:**  
Where contracts meet jurisdictional intelligence.

---

## Core Features

### Jurisdictional Intelligence Engine
- State-by-state legal rule analysis for real estate and referral compensation
- Federal overlay compliance (RESPA, FTC endorsement rules, anti-kickback frameworks)
- Continuous legal update ingestion pipeline
- Versioned rule snapshots per jurisdiction
- Regulatory vs statutory vs enforcement-pattern classification
- Legal ambiguity detection and confidence scoring

### Entity & Role Classification System
- Automatic identification of participant roles:
  - Licensed real estate agents and brokers
  - Builders and developers
  - Influencers and marketing affiliates
  - Lead generation platforms
  - Unlicensed individuals
- Role-based compliance mapping
- Cross-jurisdiction license sensitivity detection

### Compensation Structuring Engine
- Converts raw payment models into structured compliance formats
- Supports:
  - Flat advertising fees
  - Per-lead and per-click models
  - Subscription-based marketing systems
  - Affiliate marketing programs
  - Broker referral structures (licensed only)
- Detects:
  - Hidden commission systems
  - Outcome-based compensation risks
  - Transaction-contingent payment models

### Referral vs Marketing vs Brokerage Classifier
- Differentiates:
  - Legitimate referral compensation
  - Marketing and advertising services
  - Brokerage commissions
  - Kickback-risk structures
- Explainable classification reasoning
- Jurisdiction-specific classification adjustments

### Jurisdiction Rule Mapping Engine
- State-by-state legal rule database
- Licensing and compensation restrictions
- Disclosure obligations per jurisdiction
- Builder-specific compensation constraints
- Rule versioning and historical comparison tracking

# Lexara Specification Module
## Temporal Compliance Versioning (TCV) Specification
### Version 1.0

## Purpose

The Temporal Compliance Versioning (TCV) Specification defines how Lexara manages regulatory change throughout the lifecycle of legally significant agreements. Rather than replacing historical rules with current regulations, TCV preserves the complete regulatory context under which every decision, recommendation, contract, and amendment was created.

The objective is to eliminate regulatory drift by ensuring that legal reasoning remains reproducible, explainable, and auditable regardless of future legislative or regulatory changes.

---

# Design Principles

The TCV Specification is based on the following principles:

- Regulations are immutable once versioned.
- Contracts are evaluated against the rule set that governed their execution.
- Future regulations never overwrite historical legal reasoning.
- Regulatory changes are modeled as transitions rather than replacements.
- Every compliance decision must be reproducible years later.
- Human approval is required before any modification to an executed agreement.
- Regulatory history is a permanent audit artifact.

---

# Core Components

## Regulatory Version Repository

Stores every published version of:

- Federal regulations
- State regulations
- Local ordinances
- Administrative guidance
- Agency interpretations
- Industry-specific rules

Each version receives a permanent identifier.

Example:

```
Federal Anti-Kickback Statute
Version 2025.03

Effective:
2025-03-01

Superseded:
2026-08-15
```

Historical versions are never modified.

---

## Effective Date Engine

Every regulation contains:

- Published date
- Effective date
- Sunset date (if applicable)
- Transition period
- Grace period
- Retroactive applicability
- Jurisdiction

The engine determines which rule set legally applies to a given event.

---

## Contract Rule Snapshot

Every agreement generated by Lexara stores a complete snapshot containing:

- Rule set version
- Jurisdiction
- Legal authorities
- Model version
- Compliance engine version
- Risk score
- Explanation output
- Human approvals
- Supporting evidence

The snapshot remains immutable.

---

## Regulatory Transition Engine

When regulations change, Lexara creates a Regulatory Transition Event instead of replacing rules.

Example:

```
Rule Version 8
        │
        ▼
Transition Event
        │
        ▼
Rule Version 9
```

The transition records:

- What changed
- Why it changed
- Effective date
- Impacted jurisdictions
- Contracts affected
- Required actions

---

## Dual Rule Evaluation

Lexara may evaluate contracts simultaneously under:

- Historical rules
- Current rules

Result:

```
Original Compliance

✓ Compliant under Rule Set v7

Current Compliance

⚠ Requires amendment under Rule Set v8
```

This enables continuous compliance monitoring without rewriting history.

---

## Grandfathering Engine

Determines whether existing agreements remain valid after regulatory changes.

Possible outcomes:

- Grandfathered
- Amendment Required
- Immediate Noncompliance
- Administrative Notice Only
- Jurisdiction Specific Review

Every determination includes supporting legal reasoning.

---

## Amendment Recommendation Engine

Rather than modifying contracts automatically, Lexara generates:

- Amendment recommendations
- Updated clauses
- Risk analysis
- Legal justification
- Human review package

Executed contracts are never rewritten automatically.

---

## Migration Planning Engine

For regulatory updates affecting large contract portfolios, Lexara creates migration plans.

Migration plans include:

- Contracts affected
- Priority ranking
- Compliance deadlines
- Estimated legal effort
- Risk exposure
- Required approvals

---

## Regulatory Simulation Engine

Organizations may evaluate proposed regulations before implementation.

Simulation outputs include:

- Contracts impacted
- Estimated amendment workload
- Compliance costs
- Risk changes
- Operational disruptions

No production data is modified during simulation.

---

## Regulatory Event Ledger

Every regulatory change becomes a permanent ledger entry.

Each event records:

- Regulation
- Previous version
- New version
- Authority
- Publication source
- Effective date
- Jurisdiction
- Migration status

Events are append-only.

---

# Compliance States

Every contract maintains one of the following states:

- Compliant
- Pending Regulatory Review
- Grandfathered
- Amendment Recommended
- Amendment Required
- Noncompliant
- Archived

State transitions are fully auditable.

---

# Rule Evaluation Lifecycle

1. Contract created.
2. Rule snapshot stored.
3. Compliance explanation generated.
4. Human approval recorded.
5. Contract executed.
6. Regulation changes.
7. Regulatory Transition Event created.
8. Historical compliance preserved.
9. Current compliance evaluated.
10. Amendment recommendations generated if necessary.
11. Human approval required before implementation.
12. Updated snapshot recorded.

---

# Relationship to Other Lexara Specifications

The Temporal Compliance Versioning (TCV) Specification extends the Lexara Core Compliance Engine and is inherited by every industry-specific module. It does not alter substantive legal logic or jurisdictional rule mapping. Instead, it provides the temporal governance framework that ensures every compliance determination, contract, and regulatory transition remains historically accurate, explainable, and auditable throughout its lifecycle.

All future Lexara specifications that implement legal reasoning, contract generation, policy evaluation, or regulatory analysis shall conform to the requirements defined by the TCV Specification.
