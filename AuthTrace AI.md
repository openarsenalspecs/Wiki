# AuthTrace AI

**Trusted origin for licensed knowledge assets.**

AuthTrace AI is an AGPL-3.0+ governed provenance, licensing, and economic intelligence system that verifies authorship, traces content lineage, and transforms human-created knowledge into structured, licensable digital assets.

It is designed to function as an economic and trust layer for content across open networks, enabling transparent attribution, licensing, distribution, and monetization pathways while preserving creator ownership and provenance integrity.

---

# Full Feature Specification

## 1. Content Provenance & Trust Layer

- Content origin verification and authorship confidence scoring
- Content lineage graph tracking all derivatives and transformations
- Cryptographic-style content fingerprinting for uniqueness detection
- Attribution integrity enforcement across all outputs
- Content authenticity scoring system
- Audit system for licensing and attribution compliance

---

## 2. Monetization Specification Engine

- Converts any human-authored content into structured economic asset models
- Generates licensing pathways (exclusive, non-exclusive, syndication)
- Identifies derivative monetization opportunities (ebooks, courses, scripts)
- Produces pricing range models (probabilistic estimation)
- Classifies content into economic asset types
- Maps distribution and revenue potential across channels

---

## 3. Platform Compensation Intelligence Layer

- Identifies platform monetization models (ads, subscriptions, creator funds)
- Matches content formats to optimal platforms
- Provides adaptation requirements per platform (format restructuring)
- Generates monetization strength scoring per platform
- Identifies direct and indirect revenue channels

---

## 4. Distribution Strategy Engine

- Multi-platform publishing strategy generation
- Role assignment for platforms (primary revenue, discovery, amplification)
- Content sequencing optimization across channels
- Format transformation planning per distribution channel

---

## 5. Content Derivative Engine

- Automatically generates multiple content formats from one asset
- Social media transformations (threads, posts, clips)
- Newsletter and long-form expansions
- SEO and marketing variations
- Educational and script-based derivatives

---

## 6. Licensing Package Generator (AGPL-3.0+ ENFORCED)

- Generates complete Git-ready licensing repository structures
- Includes:
  - license (AGPL-3.0+)
  - notice.md (Section 7 attribution enforcement)
  - CONTENT_SPEC.json (monetization schema)
  - README.md (auto-generated documentation)
  - DERIVATIVES/ directory (generated outputs)
- Ensures attribution propagation across all derived works

---

## 7. Git Publishing Module

- Publishes generated repositories to:
  - GitHub
  - GitLab
  - Codeberg
  - self-hosted Git systems (e.g., Gitea)
- Handles repository creation, commits, and versioning

---

## 8. Licensing Outreach Intelligence Module

- Identifies industries likely to license content assets
- Maps content to enterprise and institutional use cases
- Generates buyer archetypes (education, SaaS, real estate, HR, media)
- Produces licensing opportunity scoring and value bands
- Suggests optimal license structures per industry

---

## 9. Ethical Outreach Generator

- Produces licensing outreach packets (non-automated)
- Generates:
  - email proposals
  - LinkedIn outreach drafts
  - institutional licensing briefs
- Enforces:
  - no spam automation
  - no personal data scraping
  - institutional-only targeting
  - permission-first communication model

---

## 10. Revenue Attribution & Tracking Layer

- Tracks downstream content usage and monetization
- Maps revenue flows across platforms and licenses
- Maintains attribution chain integrity
- Generates content impact and earnings graphs

## 11. Content Ownership Ledger

- Git-like versioned ledger for content lifecycle
- Tracks:
  - creation events
  - publication events
  - licensing events
  - derivative generation events
  - monetization events

## 12. Fair Deal Generator (Contract Engine)

- Generates structured licensing agreements:
  - non-exclusive licenses
  - time-limited exclusivity contracts

- Produces human-readable and structured contract formats

## 13. Monetization Simulation Engine

- Simulates multi-channel revenue scenarios
- Models platform dependency risk

- Produces probabilistic income projections (non-guaranteed)

- Exploitation Detection System

## 16. Content Provenance & Trust Layer

## 17. Economic Intelligence Layer

# System Philosophy

AuthTrace AI is designed as a **provenance-first economic infrastructure for human knowledge**.

It prioritizes:

- attribution over extraction
- transparency over opacity
- licensing over enclosure
- creator autonomy over platform dependency
- verified origin over synthetic ambiguity
