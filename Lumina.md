# Lumina

Play Smarter. Trade Safer. Shine Brighter.

Attribution: Concept by [roxanneardary.com](https://www.roxanneardary.com/)

-----

**Lumina** is an open-source universal gaming platform designed to unify the global gaming ecosystem.  
It serves as a **central discovery hub, marketplace, and community network** where players can explore games from every distributor while creators monetize their work with transparent revenue sharing.

Lumina does not replace existing gaming platforms. Instead, it **connects them**—indexing games from across the industry and linking users directly to the original distributors while also allowing developers to publish and sell games directly on Lumina.

The goal is to build the **largest open gaming directory and creator economy platform ever created.**

---

# Vision

The gaming industry is fragmented across dozens of storefronts, launchers, and marketplaces.  
Lumina solves this by creating a **unified discovery and creator platform** where:

- Players can discover **every game available worldwide**
- Developers can **publish and monetize their work**
- Communities can gather around games
- Digital assets can be traded securely
- Platforms can interconnect instead of competing in isolation

Lumina aims to become the **open index of global gaming.**

# Core Features

## Universal Game Directory

Lumina indexes games from across the gaming industry.

Each game page includes:

- Game description and metadata
- Screenshots and trailers
- Developer and publisher profiles
- Community ratings and reviews
- Tags and categories
- System requirements
- Direct links to the **original distributor or platform**

This allows players to discover games from anywhere while still respecting original distribution platforms.

---

## Creator Publishing & Monetization

Developers can publish games directly to Lumina with built-in monetization tools.

Features include:

- Creator storefront pages
- Game publishing tools
- Built-in revenue sharing
- Configurable profit splits for collaborators
- Automated royalty distribution
- Analytics dashboards
- Update and patch management
- Early access and beta testing programs

Lumina simply takes a **small hosting percentage** from sales.

---

## Profit Sharing System

Revenue splitting is integrated into the platform from the start.

Creators can define profit shares for:

- Developers
- Artists
- Sound designers
- Writers
- Publishers
- Collaborators

Payments are automatically distributed based on the defined percentages.

---

## Global Payments

Lumina supports payments worldwide.

Supported systems include:

- Global fiat currencies
- Cryptocurrency wallets
- Multi-currency user wallets
- Peer-to-peer transactions
- Digital asset ownership transfers
- Marketplace trading

Users choose their preferred payment method and currency.

---

## Peer-to-Peer Digital Asset Trading

Players can securely trade:

- In-game items

Transactions are encrypted and verified.
