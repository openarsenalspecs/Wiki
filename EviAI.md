# 🌟 Overview

**EviAI** is a **self-contained, end-to-end encrypted, offline-capable trusted intelligence platform** designed for individuals and organizations that require:

- Privacy
- Transparency
- Verifiable answers
- Complete data ownership
- Secure AI execution
- Long-term knowledge preservation

Unlike cloud-dependent AI assistants, EviAI is designed as a modular local-first system that combines:

- AI reasoning
- Source verification
- Encrypted memory
- Knowledge management
- Secure execution
- Multi-agent intelligence
- Extensible plugins

EviAI transforms AI from a simple chatbot into a **private intelligence infrastructure layer**.
