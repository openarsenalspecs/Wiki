# AuthorityCore

**AuthorityCore** is an AGPL 3.0+ licensed focus-oriented AI governance, reliability, and execution framework designed to improve long-horizon task completion through objective contracts, scope compression, jurisdiction-aware reasoning, evidence-constrained retrieval, human oversight, and full workflow traceability.

**AuthorityCore**  
**Jurisdiction Matters.**

---

## Overview

AuthorityCore is built on a foundational principle:

> Reliability in AI systems is achieved by reducing possibility space, not expanding it.

Instead of allowing unconstrained model reasoning across all possible knowledge domains, AuthorityCore progressively narrows scope based on:

- Objective definition
- Jurisdiction selection
- Authority hierarchy
- Source constraints
- Human approval checkpoints

The system ensures that every output remains aligned with an approved objective and traceable to verified sources.

---

## Core Principles

- **Human Authority First** – Humans define and approve objectives, scope, and conclusions.
- **Focus Orientation** – Reduce possibility space until only relevant knowledge remains.
- **Jurisdiction Matters** – Legal and regulated domains are always jurisdiction-bound.
- **Evidence Over Probability** – All outputs must trace to verifiable sources.
- **Immutable Traceability** – Every decision is logged and reproducible.
- **Controlled Autonomy** – AI operates only within approved constraints.

## Governance Model

AuthorityCore is governed by an internal constitutional framework:

- Human authority is primary
- Jurisdiction must always be defined
- Scope expansion requires approval
- Sources must be traceable
- Evidence is required for claims
- Conflicts must be disclosed
- Audit logs must be preserved
- Drift must be detected and corrected
