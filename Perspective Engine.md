# Perspective Engine

**Stop echoing trends. Start reframing them.**

Perspective Engine is a modular, human-in-the-loop intelligence system that detects trending topics across local and industry signals, evaluates saturation and narrative repetition, and transforms information into differentiated, niche-aligned content perspectives for creators.

It does not generate generic content. It engineers original angles.

---

## 🔥 Core Features

### 📡 Multi-Source Signal Ingestion
- RSS feeds, news outlets, blogs, newsletters
- Social signals (Reddit, YouTube trends, approved APIs)
- Google Trends and keyword velocity tracking
- User-defined watchlists (topics, URLs, competitors)
- Normalized cross-source data ingestion pipeline

---

### 🧹 Signal Cleaning & Normalization
- Deduplication across all sources
- Spam and low-signal filtering
- Entity extraction (people, places, companies, events)
- Geo-tagging for local relevance mapping
- Standardized signal schema for processing

---

### 🧠 Trend Detection Engine
- Topic clustering across sources
- Emerging vs declining narrative detection
- Momentum scoring (velocity and acceleration)
- Cross-platform trend correlation
- Local vs regional vs global classification

---

### 📊 Content Saturation & Echo Detection
- Detects overused narratives in real time
- Measures content overcrowding per topic
- Identifies repetitive framing patterns across creators
- Applies saturation penalties to recommendations
- Maintains global “echo index” system

---

### 🎯 Creator Relevance Intelligence Layer
- Creator profile modeling (niche, geography, tone, audience type)
- Industry alignment scoring (real estate, tech, finance, lifestyle, etc.)
- Local relevance weighting based on geographic context
- Audience sophistication matching (beginner → expert)
- Personalized ranking of all topic opportunities

---

### 🧭 Perspective & Differentiation Engine
- Prevents duplicate content recommendations
- Forces unique framing per topic
- Generates multiple angle variations per idea
- Perspective shifts:
  - consumer, investor, expert, policy, emotional, analytical
- Format shifts:
  - blog, video script, carousel, newsletter, explainer
- Narrative shifts:
  - contrarian takes, myth-busting, case studies, timelines

---

### 🧠 Content Memory System
- Stores creator content history
- Prevents repetition of past topics
- Semantic similarity detection across content
- Global registry of overused narrative patterns
- Duplicate prevention before recommendation

---

### 🧭 Human-in-the-Loop Interface
- Ranked “reframed perspective” feed
- Transparent scoring (relevance, novelty, saturation)
- Multi-angle suggestion cards per topic
- Explanation of why each angle is unique
- Creator selection, rejection, and feedback controls

---

### ✍️ Content Structuring Assistant (Optional)
- Generates outlines after topic selection
- Hook, title, and opening line suggestions
- Multi-tone content variations
- Platform-specific formatting (blog, TikTok, LinkedIn)
- Expands selected perspective into structured draft

---

### 🔁 Feedback Learning System
- Learns from user selections and rejections
- Tracks engagement performance (optional integrations)
- Dynamically adjusts relevance weighting
- Improves niche alignment over time
- Continuously refines novelty scoring

---

### 🔌 Modular Plugin Architecture
- Fully pluggable ingestion system
- Swappable AI providers (local or external LLMs)
- Independent scoring and ranking modules
- Extensible connector ecosystem
- Plugin manifest system for activation

---

### 🧾 Transparency & Explainability Layer
- Full reasoning trace per recommendation
- Displays relevance, saturation, and novelty scores
- Shows blocked or excluded angles
- Source traceability for all signals
- Fully auditable decision pipeline

---

### 🧱 Scoring & Ranking Engine
Each topic is evaluated using:

- Local relevance score
- Niche alignment score
- Trend momentum score
- Saturation / echo penalty score
- Creator history similarity score
- Novelty enforcement score

Final output: ranked list of **undercapitalized perspectives**

---

### 🧑‍💻 Creator Profile System
- Multi-niche support per user
- Geo-targeting (city, region, global)
- Audience modeling
- Platform-specific optimization profiles
- Tone and voice preference mapping

---

### 🧠 Anti-Echo Chamber System
- Detects overused narratives across ecosystems
- Prevents recommendation clustering around viral sameness
- Promotes underrepresented perspectives
- Enforces narrative diversity
- Continuously recalibrates saturation baselines

---

### 📈 Opportunity Discovery Engine
- Identifies low-competition, high-relevance topics
- Surfaces emerging local/community narratives early
- Detects cross-industry intersections
- Highlights underreported stories
- Prioritizes attention gaps in information ecosystems
