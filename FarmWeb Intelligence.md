# FarmWeb Intelligence

**A transparent network connecting farms, ownership, and public resources.**

FarmWeb Intelligence is an open-source agricultural intelligence platform designed to map farms, agricultural businesses, ownership structures, public funding, and food production networks into a unified intelligence system.

The platform creates a transparent relationship graph connecting agricultural operations with corporate filings, subsidiaries, public agents, government programs, legislation, and publicly available economic data. Its purpose is to help citizens, researchers, policymakers, and organizations understand where food comes from, who controls agricultural assets, and how public resources flow through the food system.

---

# Project Goals

FarmWeb Intelligence is designed to:

- Create a transparent map of agricultural operations
- Connect farms with their legal business structures
- Identify parent companies, subsidiaries, and related entities
- Track public funding and agricultural assistance programs
- Link funding sources to the legislation and programs that created them
- Build searchable profiles of agricultural organizations and public agents
- Provide an open foundation for agricultural transparency worldwide

---

# Modular Architecture

FarmWeb Intelligence uses a modular architecture where essential capabilities are maintained as core modules, while additional capabilities can be added through optional plugin modules.

The modular approach allows communities, researchers, governments, and developers to deploy only the capabilities they need while maintaining a scalable foundation for future expansion.
