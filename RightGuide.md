# RightGuide

**RightGuide — Your Compass in Legal Matters**

RightGuide is an open-source AI-powered legal analysis platform that helps individuals understand laws that may apply to a situation based on their location. Users describe a situation in plain language, provide a location, and receive a clear explanation of applicable local, state, and federal laws, potential legal concerns, recommended actions, reporting options, and legal assistance resources.

The project is designed to make legal information more accessible while promoting responsible decision-making through plain-language explanations and verifiable legal references.

## Important Notice

RightGuide is an informational and educational platform.

RightGuide is not a law firm and does not provide legal representation. Information provided by the platform should not be considered legal advice. Users should consult a licensed attorney regarding specific legal matters and legal strategies.

Legal outcomes depend on specific facts, jurisdictional requirements, judicial interpretation, and changes in applicable laws and regulations.
