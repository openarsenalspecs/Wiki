## Purpose

CreatorGrid is an open source AI assistant specification designed to protect creators and strengthen the legal infrastructure of the creator economy.

CreatorGrid provides a modular framework for analyzing creator rights, copyright, contracts, licensing, jurisdiction, artificial intelligence use, compensation, ownership, termination, reversion, platform policies, and potential circumvention of legal protections.

The system is designed to help creators understand what rights may apply to their work, what rights a contract may attempt to transfer or restrict, which jurisdictions may be materially relevant, and where existing legal or contractual frameworks may contain protection gaps.

CreatorGrid is designed to identify potential risks and legal questions without automatically treating a potential loophole, contractual provision, or jurisdictional difference as unlawful conduct.

---

## Design Principles

### Creator Sovereignty

Creators should remain informed and in control of consequential decisions involving their creative works, rights, contracts, licenses, assignments, artificial intelligence use, and disputes.

### Rights First

The system should identify creator rights before evaluating contractual restrictions, waivers, assignments, licenses, or other limitations.

### Jurisdiction Awareness

The system must identify jurisdictions materially connected to the creator, creative work, contract, transaction, operator, platform, license, and exploitation of the work.

### Contractual Transparency

Contracts must be analyzed according to their substantive effects rather than relying solely on titles, labels, or individual clauses.

### Anti-Circumvention

The system should identify potential structures that may weaken, avoid, fragment, relocate, or otherwise circumvent creator protections.

### Prior Art Awareness

The system should continuously examine legal, technical, contractual, patent, regulatory, and policy prior art for protection gaps and potential loopholes.

### Evidence Before Conclusions

Significant conclusions should be supported by identifiable evidence, legal authority, dates, jurisdictions, provenance, and confidence assessments.

### Human Control

The system must preserve human control over consequential legal, contractual, licensing, and rights decisions.

### Legal Uncertainty

The system must distinguish established law from unsettled law, conflicting authority, potential loopholes, interpretations, and questions requiring professional legal review.

### Open Protection

CreatorGrid should support interoperable, portable, machine-readable creator-rights information without requiring creators to surrender ownership or control to a particular provider.
